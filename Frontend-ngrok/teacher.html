🔲 Barcode kya hota hai?

Ek unique number jo har product ko identify karta hai

1

Barcode = Product ka Aadhar Card

Jaise har insaan ka Aadhar number alag hota hai, waise hi har product ka barcode alag hota hai.

Amul Butter 500g → 8901030868725
Parle-G 800g → 8901719110017
Cadbury DM 40g → 7622210449061
2

Kahan milega barcode?

Product ke packet ke peeche — black lines wala box. Scanner se scan karo ya manually type karo.

3

ERP mein barcode kaise kaam karta hai?

POS mein scan karo barcode
↓
System dhundhta hai: barcode = 8901030868725
↓
Milta hai: Amul Butter 500g — ₹55
↓
Cart mein add ✅
Agar barcode nahi hai product pe → khud ek number do jaise "AMB001" — koi bhi unique code chalega.

📦 SUPPLIER NE MAAL DIYA
  ↓
Step 1: Products mein add karo (pehli baar)
        Name + Barcode + Price + GST
  ↓
Step 2: Inventory → Stock IN
        Product select + Qty = 5 → Save
        → DB: stock_qty = 5 ✅
  ↓
🛒 CUSTOMER AAYA
  ↓
Step 3: POS → Barcode scan
        → Cart mein add
        → Payment: Cash/UPI/Card
        → Checkout
  ↓
DB Update:
        orders table → new bill ✅
        products table → stock_qty - 1 ✅
        stock_movements → OUT record ✅
  ↓
📊 DASHBOARD UPDATE → sales, stock, payments sab live
